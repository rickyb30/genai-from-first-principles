# Code of Conduct

This project exists to help people learn. Everyone who takes part (learners, contributors,
reviewers and maintainers) should be able to do so without harassment or exclusion,
whatever their background, identity, experience level or first language.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with beginners. Every question is a fair question.
- Assume good intent. Ask for clarification before you assume the worst.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person. Be specific and suggest improvements.
- Accept correction gracefully. Accuracy matters more than being right.
- Credit other people's work and ideas.
- Write for a global audience. Avoid idioms, slang and in-jokes that exclude people.

## What we don't accept

- Harassment, intimidation, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Personal attacks, insults or sustained disruption of discussions.
- Sexualized language or imagery.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their permission.
- Using this project to promote a product or vendor, or to disparage one.
- Any other conduct a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews) and
whenever someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainer, Ricky Brar, at
[meetrickyb@gmail.com](mailto:meetrickyb@gmail.com). Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers who are involved in
a report will step aside from handling it.

## Enforcement

Maintainers will review every report and respond in proportion to what happened. Responses
range from a private note asking for a change in behaviour, to removing content, to a temporary
or permanent ban from the project. Maintainers who don't follow this code in good faith may be
removed from the maintainer team.
