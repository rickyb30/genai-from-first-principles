    Learning paths

    Curated routes through the catalog for different roles. Each path tells you which lessons to read and how deep to go in each: 🟢 core only, 🟡 core + practitioner, or 🔴 everything.

    Draft. These paths follow the approved outline. Lesson links are added as lessons are published, and the paths are refined in the final polish phase. Lesson IDs match CURRICULUM.md.

    Not sure where you stand? Take the placement self-assessment in assessments/ first.

    Business leader or non-technical professional

    Goal: understand what generative AI can and can't do, judge proposals, and ask the right questions about risk and value. No code required. About 15 hours.

    Step Lessons Depth
    1. Orientation L0-01 to L0-05 🟢
    2. How models generate L1-02, L2-07, L2-09, L2-10, L2-11, L2-12 🟢
    3. What you can build L3-02, L3-08, L3-13, L4-01, L4-08, L4-09 🟢
    4. Running it responsibly L5-01, L5-08, L5-10 🟢
    5. Risk and governance L6-01, L6-02, L6-08 to L6-12 🟢
    6. Where it's heading L7-01, L7-11 🟢
