# Level 7 — Expert & Frontier Topics

Expert and frontier topics: reasoning models, mixture-of-experts, long context, alternative architectures, training at scale, reinforcement learning, interpretability and the science of evaluation.

**12 lessons · about 12.8 hours** of reading and code, plus exercises.

## Learning objectives

By the end of this level you will be able to:

- Explain test-time compute, mixture-of-experts and long-context techniques.
- Explain how reinforcement learning shapes language models.
- Apply basic interpretability methods to TinyLM.
- Critique benchmark claims and design an enterprise platform.
