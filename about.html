    About this site

    What this is

    Generative AI from First Principles is an open, vendor-neutral catalog that teaches generative AI from no prior knowledge to expert level. This site is an interactive way to read it. Every page is built from the same Markdown files you can read on GitHub, so the two never disagree.

    New modules are released regularly. The curriculum shows what's available and what's coming.

    Privacy

    • No accounts, no cookies, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party scripts or fonts.
    • Your progress (completed lessons, quiz scores, flashcard schedule and settings) is saved in your browser's local storage, on this device only. It never leaves your device.
    • You can export, import or delete it at any time on the progress page.

    Vendor-neutral by design

    The catalog teaches concepts, patterns and open standards, not products. Code examples run offline, with no accounts or keys.
